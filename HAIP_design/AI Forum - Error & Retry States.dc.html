Generation failures — the six states

claude -p will fail, so "drafting…" forever can't be the only outcome. The many technical failures collapse into a handful of UX patterns — A failed → retry · B rate-limited → wait → retry · C cancelled (a choice, not a failure) · D partial failure inside a roomful · E couldn't save · F pre-send validation. Every Retry is owner-tapped in M1.
